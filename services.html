01 / Independent product review

Find out what's actually wrong.

Request a product review ↗

A release keeps slipping. The vendor says the bug is nearly fixed. Your customers are seeing issues, but you can't tell whether the proposed solution is right. That's where an independent review helps.

We get access to your product, code and data, speak with the people building it and review what the next release actually depends on. The result is a candid readout of what is fragile, what can wait and what to fix first.

What we look at

The live product, source code, data, architecture, vendors, engineering capacity and planned releases.

What you leave with

A plain-English assessment of the risks and a prioritized, practical roadmap your team can use.
