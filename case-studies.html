01 / Vantage Peptides
Visit Vantage Peptides ↗

Orders were growing. Fulfillment was holding the business back.

The situation

Monthly online revenue was around $2,000, while manual fulfillment and an inconsistent buying experience were taking time away from customer acquisition and sales.

What changed

Over nine months, the work improved the product offering, ecommerce experience and order fulfillment. Monthly online revenue grew to more than $15,000, giving the team more time to focus on customers and sales.

02 / Confidential connected-fitness company

The product was ambitious. It needed one workable plan.

The situation

Launching a connected-fitness product meant coordinating hardware, software, marketing and customer growth. Each part had its own dependencies and deadlines.

What changed

The engagement brought those workstreams into one product roadmap and supported the path from an early investment of roughly $1.9 million to a reported $10 million in annual recurring revenue.
