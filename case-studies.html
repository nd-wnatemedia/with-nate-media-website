01 / Vantage Peptides
Visit Vantage Peptides ↗

The business was growing. The process wasn't.

The situation

The online store was generating roughly $2,000 a month, but fulfillment required too much manual involvement and the customer experience wasn't bringing people back consistently. Too much energy was going into processing orders instead of creating new ones.

What changed

Over nine months, the engagement covered the product offering, ecommerce experience, and operations behind the sale. Monthly online revenue grew to more than $15,000 and the sales team had more space to focus on selling.

02 / Confidential connected-fitness company

A good idea still needs a path to market.

The situation

A connected home-fitness product had to move from concept to launch—with physical hardware, technology, marketing, and increasingly ambitious customer-growth targets all needing to work together.

What changed

We helped establish the technical, hardware, and marketing roadmap, mapped growth milestones from 100 customers toward 100,000, and supported the journey from a roughly $1.9M investment to a reported $10M ARR business.

03 / LAVA
Visit LAVA ↗

Stop building everything. Build the right thing.

The situation

A sports-and-entertainment platform needed a clearer product direction and a better way to turn its core capabilities into commercial value.

What changed

We refocused the roadmap on the product's core tenets and helped launch a sponsorship marketplace feature. The initiative represented an estimated $3M incremental revenue opportunity.

04 / Confidential mental-wellness app

The MVP was live. That didn't mean it was ready.

The situation

A wellness app had been pushed into production before the technical foundations were ready for growth. The systems were fragile, and each new user brought more operational risk.

What changed

We evaluated the technology and established an infrastructure and scaling roadmap. Over the next 90 days, the business grew from approximately 150 to 2,000 daily active users, alongside a reported tenfold revenue increase.
